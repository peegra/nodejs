# Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you book a hotel, tour or other service through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked with a note at the top of the page.

We don't accept payment to recommend shops, and affiliate relationships don't change what we write about where to find cards.
