# Where to look for Pokémon cards in Tokyo

Different kinds of shops are good for different things. Knowing which is which saves you a lot of walking.

## Pokémon Center stores

The official stores sell Pokémon Center exclusives, promos and merchandise, plus cards when stock allows. Tokyo has several, including large stores in Ikebukuro and Nihonbashi and one in Shibuya. [[VERIFY: 東京のポケモンセンターの店舗一覧と所在地（移転・閉店がないか）]]

**Best for:** exclusives and merchandise. **Don't expect:** new booster boxes on release day.

## Card shop districts: Akihabara and Nakano

Specialty card shops sell **singles** (individual cards) and sometimes older sealed products. Prices are market prices, not retail, but this is where you'll find specific cards you want.

- **Akihabara** has a dense cluster of card shops within a few blocks.
- **Nakano Broadway** is a shopping complex full of collectible and hobby shops.

[[VERIFY: 秋葉原・中野ブロードウェイのカードショップの状況（2026年時点）]]

**Best for:** singles and older sets. **Tip:** compare prices between a few shops before buying.

## Big electronics stores

Large electronics and toy stores carry trading cards on their toy floors. New releases are often sold by lottery or in limited quantities.

**Best for:** checking for older sets and accessories (sleeves, binders).

## Convenience stores

Convenience stores sometimes stock packs, but deliveries are small and irregular, and per-checkout limits are common. Treat a find as a lucky bonus, not a plan.

**Please don't** go store-to-store asking staff about delivery times — they get asked constantly and generally can't say.
