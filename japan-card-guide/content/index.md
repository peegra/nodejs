# Hunting for Pokémon cards in Japan

Japanese Pokémon cards are a favorite souvenir — but buying them in Japan is not as simple as walking into a shop. New sets sell out fast, many stores limit how much you can buy, and the official lotteries are hard for visitors to enter.

This guide helps you plan realistically:

- **[The 2026 buying rules](/buying-rules/)** — purchase limits, lotteries, kids-only sales, and tax-free shopping.
- **[Where to look in Tokyo](/where-to-buy-tokyo/)** — Pokémon Centers, card shop districts, and big electronics stores.
- **[Release calendar](/release-calendar/)** — upcoming Japanese releases and retail prices.
- **[A one-day card-hunting plan](/itinerary/)** — a route you can actually do in a day.

> We're not affiliated with The Pokémon Company or any shop. We can't tell you where stock will be on a given day — nobody honestly can — but we can help you avoid wasted trips.
