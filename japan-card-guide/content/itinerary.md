# A one-day card-hunting plan for Tokyo

This route mixes the official store, card shop districts and a little sightseeing, so the day is fun even if you don't find the set you wanted.

## Morning: Pokémon Center

Start at a Pokémon Center when it opens to look for exclusives and merchandise. Weekday mornings are calmer than weekends. Some stores limit entry on busy days, so check the [official store list](https://www.pokemon.co.jp/shop/en/) for hours and notices first.

## Midday: Akihabara

Spend a few hours in Akihabara's card shops comparing prices on singles and older sets. Grab lunch nearby — the area is full of quick options.

## Afternoon: Nakano Broadway

Head to Nakano Broadway for collectibles and hobby shops. It's a good place to find older or unusual items.

## Evening: rest near your hotel

Staying near Akihabara or along the Yamanote Line keeps travel time short.
