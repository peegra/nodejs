# About Japan Card Hunt

Japan Card Hunt is written from Japan by a local who follows Japanese-language announcements so visitors don't have to. We focus on practical, honest information: what the rules are, where it's worth looking, and how to enjoy the trip even when shelves are empty.

We are not affiliated with The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Bandai, or any retailer.
